Statuses
  Strength: +1 PWR per stack.
  Vitality: +1 HP per stack.
  Curse: -1 PWR per stack.
  Poison: Turn end: damage equal to stacks to holder, then spend 1 stack.
  Shield: Would be hit: block damage up to stacks, spending them.
  Freeze: Would strike: cancel it, spend 1 stack.
  Blessing: Would die: cancel death, set holder to HP equal to stacks, spend this status.
