Arena of Ideas sound effects

The sounds come from Duamo (makscee/duamo, Assets/Sounds), Maks's own game,
the same game as the music themes (void-board#922 keeps one family: Duamo's
warm synth sounds sit with its themes; the brighter Arena of Ideas 2022 set
was dropped):
- heal is the exception: a synthesised two-note chime (docs/mission1007/make-heal-sfx.py),
  so a heal never sounds like a buff.
- discover (field_complete, cut to 2.6 s), draw (field_close), freeze
  (shape_sides_close), fuse (insert_main), move-left / move-right
  (move_attached_left/right_1), reroll (move_attached_rotate_left_1), start
  (field_open), status (insert_start), status-off (undo), turn
  (move_attached_rotate_right_1), unfreeze (shape_sides_open), wrong
  (insert_wrong).

Converted to mono MP3 (trimmed, peak-normalised) from the originals; the
older ones are listed in docs/round3/sounds/web-map.txt. Which cue plays
which file, at what rate and gain: mobile/ui/sounds.json, levelled by
docs/mission5/sound/level.py.
